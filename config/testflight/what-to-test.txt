Before testing, export a backup of your Actual budget.

What to test:
- Connect to your server, import a budget, and compare Budget, Spending, Accounts, and Reports with Actual.
- Create, edit, categorize, split, transfer, and delete test transactions.
- Assign and move budget money, then check that changes appear in Actual.
- Make a small change offline, reconnect, and check that it syncs without missing or duplicate transactions.
- Explore this build's changes, your preferred theme and display density, and widgets.

Report problems through Settings → Support → Share Diagnostic Report. Include what happened, what you expected, and the steps to reproduce it. Remove personal information from screenshots before sharing.

Known limitations:
- Bank Sync supports SimpleFIN only. Other providers require another Actual client or your server. Background Bank Sync is experimental and off by default; enabling it saves downloaded changes automatically.
- Account reconciliation is not available. You can add accounts, but renaming, closing, reopening, and deleting accounts are not yet supported.
- Creating, renaming, deleting, and reordering categories still require another Actual client.
- Some imported rules, including split rules, cannot be edited here.
- Templates written in category notes remain read-only. Some advanced template types and options cannot be edited here.
