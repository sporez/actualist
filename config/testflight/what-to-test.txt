Actualist {{VERSION}} ({{BUILD}}) — Initial Beta

Before testing, export a backup of your Actual budget.

Please test:
- Connect to your Actual server, select a budget, and complete the initial import.
- Compare Budget, Spending, Accounts, and Reports with the official Actual client.
- Create, edit, categorize, split, transfer, and delete test transactions.
- Assign and move budget money, then confirm changes sync back to Actual.
- Launch offline, review cached data, make a small change, reconnect, and confirm it syncs without duplicates or missing data.
- Try the app icon, display density, theme, refresh, and background-sync settings.

Report problems through Settings → Support → Share Diagnostic Report. Include reproduction steps and redacted screenshots.

Known limitations:
- Bank Sync supports SimpleFIN only. Other providers still need another Actual client or the server.
- Account reconciliation is not available.
- Creating, renaming, closing, and deleting accounts is incomplete.
- Budget templates support creating and editing the current Fixed Amount, Save
  by Date, Percentage, Balance Limit, Refill, Copy, Average, Schedule, Remainder,
  and Goal catalog. Category-note templates and unsupported newer shapes remain
  view-only.
