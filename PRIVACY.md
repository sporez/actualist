# Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026.

## We Collect Nothing

The Actualist project and its developer do not collect, receive, keep, sell, or
share your data. Actualist has no user accounts, cloud service, analytics,
advertising, tracking, or developer-operated backend.

The project website is static. It does not set cookies or collect analytics.

## Your Data Stays With You

Actualist keeps a local copy of your budget on your device and syncs directly
with the Actual Budget server you choose. Your saved sign-in details and
budget encryption keys stay in the system Keychain.

Optional connections run only when you choose them and contact only your server,
a service you configure, or an Apple-provided system picker. The developer does
not receive that data. App extensions use limited on-device data without your
sign-in details, and notifications are generated locally.

## Exports

Files you export leave the app only through the iOS share sheet, to the place
you pick. An exported budget ZIP is not encrypted, so share it only with people
and places you trust.

## Services You Choose

Your server and any identity, financial-data, hosting, or other service you
choose have their own privacy practices. Actualist does not operate them.

Apple may collect beta usage, crash, and diagnostic information when you use
TestFlight. Information you submit in a GitHub report is handled by GitHub.
Review attachments and never include passwords or financial details.

## Backups and Deletion

Local budget data, changes waiting to upload, recovery copies, and shared
extension data are excluded from device backups. Synced changes can be restored
from your Actual server. Changes still waiting to upload cannot.

Use **Disconnect and Erase Data** in Actualist to remove local budgets, saved
sign-in details, and shared extension data. Use it before uninstalling so
nothing is left in the Keychain. Erasing data on this device does not delete
anything from your server or revoke access at other services.

## Contact

For a privacy question, open a GitHub issue that contains no sensitive
information. For a security issue or a report containing sensitive details,
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
